About

DJ Fatoom

With an ear for elegance and a pulse on the crowd, DJ Fatoom transforms events into unforgettable sonic journeys. Specializing in luxury weddings, private soirées, and high-energy club nights, Fatoom blends genres seamlessly — from Balearic sunset vibes to deep, driving house. As the official DJ partner of BAZEEVENT, every set is curated to elevate the atmosphere and create memories that last a lifetime.

Each set is a journey — crafted to the moment, the crowd, and the space.
